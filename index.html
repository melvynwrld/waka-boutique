WAKA
Our Story

Made in Uzunguni, worn everywhere.

Waka began with a simple question: why does African fabric so often get treated as a trend, and not as craft?

We work with local fabric artisans and tailors in Dodoma. Every piece is cut, sewn, and finished in our studio. Nothing is mass-produced. Nothing is rushed.

The result is a wardrobe that carries its making — and yours. Clothes you'll wear for years, not seasons.
